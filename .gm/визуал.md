Muted desaturated palette, cold blues/greens of Eastern European marshes and forests, warm firelight accents. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, volumetric fog, film grain, natural skin texture. Slavic medieval architecture and clothing. No cartoon, no anime, no text.
